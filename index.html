Why it exists

A client for people who do not want the ceiling decided for them.

Vencord built a strong foundation. Equicord widened the plugin catalog. Illegalcord is what happens when that foundation is treated like a launchpad instead of a fence.

Credit where it is due: Illegalcord exists because the work of Vencord, Equicord, and their communities made this direction possible. Respect for the base. No respect for artificial limits.

The debate is simple: Vencord and Equicord optimize for approval. Illegalcord optimizes for potential.

Disclaimer

Provocative name. Not an illegal client.

Illegalcord does not endorse illegal activity, abuse, harassment, credential theft, malware, or platform harm. Sensitive features are presented as proof-of-concept, privacy, research, customization, or educational experiments.

Modified clients can break, can be unstable, and can violate Discord's Terms of Service. That risk exists for Vencord, Equicord, Illegalcord, and every other modified Discord client. Use your own judgment, inspect the source, and avoid unofficial builds you cannot verify.

FAQ

Questions people ask when a client stops asking for permission.

What might the developers of Vencord and Equicord think of this project?

They may dislike it. They may call it unstable, risky, too permissive, or tell users to stay away. Some caution is fair: modified clients can break, violate Discord's Terms of Service, and create problems if users install random builds without understanding them. The difference is philosophical. Illegalcord believes users should see the full potential, understand the risk, and choose for themselves. Vencord and Equicord deserve credit for the foundation; Illegalcord rejects the ceiling.

Is Illegalcord better than Vencord or Equicord?

If you want the safest-looking, most conservative modded client, choose the conservative option. If you want higher-quality streaming experiments, microphone control, stereo audio, stronger privacy PoCs, and a plugin catalog that is not afraid of uncomfortable ideas, Illegalcord is the sharper tool.

Isn't Vencord more stable?

In practice, Illegalcord is built to be more stable for power users because it gives them tools to understand the client instead of guessing. Updates can rarely have a 1-2 day delay when Discord changes something, but the client is not just "more plugins and hope." Illegalcord includes Client Diagnostics, a plugin that lets users see which plugin or surface is causing lag, slow calls, memory pressure, or active background work. It also includes CrashHandlerEnhanced, a required crash recovery and logging layer that records reports, writes crash logs, detects suspected plugins, and can recover or isolate the problem. Vencord and Equicord may look more conservative; Illegalcord gives you more control over stability.

Do you censor the names of other modified Discord clients?

No. Illegalcord does not censor the names of modified Discord clients, and we will not pretend Vencord, Equicord, or any other client cannot be named just because someone wants to frame them as unstable or forbidden to discuss. We believe in real freedom of speech: name the clients, compare the features, inspect the source, and let users decide. If another project needs silence to defend its limits, that is not our problem.

Why not submit these features to Vencord or Equicord?

Because the disagreement is philosophical. Some features do not fit their rules, image, or comfort zone. Illegalcord exists because not every useful idea should be rejected just because it makes a conservative project uncomfortable. Their rules are their rules; Illegalcord is for users who want the ideas those rules leave outside.

Is Illegalcord malware?

The project is open source. Do not trust random binaries from strangers, do not install builds you cannot verify, and do inspect the source when possible. Calling every ambitious modified client malware is an easy way to scare users back into limited choices; verification is better than fear.

Why should I trust Illegalcord instead of Vencord or Equicord?

You can trust it, or you can choose not to. The real question is: why did you trust them? Most users install Vencord or Equicord without reading a single line of their source code. Illegalcord does not ask for blind faith. Read the source code, inspect the client, inspect the website, compare it with every other client, and make your own decision. Illegalcord was originally built for myself because I wanted a better Discord experience; then I kept improving it, and people started liking the project. If you do not trust it, do not use it. Use the limited client you prefer. That is not an insult to us. Users are free to choose the modified Discord client they want.

Is Illegalcord illegal?

No. Illegalcord is not an illegal client, and the name is not an invitation to commit crimes or harm anyone. The "illegal" part is about breaking the pointless limits, unwritten rules, and closed-vision mindset around Vencord and Equicord. They built valuable foundations, but they also keep Discord modding inside a smaller box than it needs to be. Illegalcord exists to push past that box and unlock the real potential of a modified Discord client.

Can Discord ban me for using Illegalcord?

Yes, any modified Discord client can be against Discord's Terms of Service and can put your account at risk. That is true for Vencord, Equicord, Illegalcord, and other client mods. If you are already accepting that risk, Illegalcord argues you might as well use the client that gives you the strongest experience.

Why does Illegalcord include experimental or sensitive plugins?

Because technical users learn by testing boundaries. Illegalcord frames sensitive functionality as PoC, research, privacy, customization, and educational work. The project does not endorse abuse. The goal is to understand what modified clients can do and give users control over their own setup.

Do you respect Vencord and Equicord?

Yes. Illegalcord is possible because their work exists. The thanks are real. The disagreement is also real: a foundation can be respected while its limits are challenged.

Is Illegalcord vibe-coded? Did you use AI?

Yes, AI has been used for some parts of the work. That does not make Illegalcord skidded, stolen, fake, or low quality. AI-assisted coding is a tool, not a confession. Quality comes from what is reviewed, tested, maintained, and shipped openly. The same people who attack a Discord client for using AI still use apps, operating systems, IDEs, cloud platforms, and services maintained by companies using AI-assisted engineering: Spotify has publicly discussed Honk powered by Claude Code, Microsoft and GitHub push Copilot, Amazon built CodeWhisperer and Amazon Q Developer, Replit built Ghostwriter and AI agents, and Google and Meta have both talked about AI-assisted development. If someone rejects Illegalcord only because AI helped with parts of it, they should be consistent and reject the modern software stack too. Otherwise, it is not a principle; it is selective outrage.
